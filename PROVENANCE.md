# Provenance

Catalog Q311 points to the non-strict threshold variation in §6.4 of Pavlos Marantidis's dissertation. The frozen discovery packet and recovered five-page manuscript contain the same finite-controller strategy. The revised manuscript credits the exact earlier infimum algorithm and the dissertation's proposed optimal-transition construction. It keeps the original theorem boundary and presents an additional paired positive-infimum example. Tests in this archive report the supplied runnable checks.
