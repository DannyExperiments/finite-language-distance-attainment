The paper decides the non-strict $d_2$ threshold for finite language assignments, with finite constants, Boolean operations and one-sided concatenation by finite constants. It proves the optimal-transition characterization needed to complete Marantidis's proposed finite-attainment construction in §6.4.

Internally reviewed, not peer reviewed. The exact replay provides finite computational corroboration.
